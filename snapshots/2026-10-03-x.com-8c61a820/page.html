    Why do we do this? LLMs are trained to refuse harmful tasks e.g. related to making chemical weapons. But this safety training can be overridden with jailbreaks. Right now every LLM is vulnerable.
    5
    Jan Leike
    @janleike
    Feb 3, 2025
    We add two classifiers on top of an LLM to block inputs and outputs. Jailbreaking our system requires jailbreaking all three models at the same time: the LLM and our two classifiers.
    6
    Jan Leike
    @janleike
    Feb 3, 2025
    Our goal is to defend against universal jailbreaks that unlock the model on many queries at once, because they enable repeated misuse. We believe that finding new jailbreaks for every query is annoying enough that most people will give up.
    5
    Jan Leike
    @janleike
    Feb 3, 2025
    Experts tried breaking an earlier prototype for >3,000 hours and no one found a universal jailbreak. But our system isn't perfect, and it's still possible to jailbreak the model on single queries.
