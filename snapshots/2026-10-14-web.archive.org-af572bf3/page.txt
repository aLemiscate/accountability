OpenAI Fires Researchers for Allegedly Sharing Information with AI Safety Group
The startup, in the throes of responding to rogue AI incidents, said three employees violated policies for handling sensitive company data
By
Keach Hagey ,
Maxwell Zeff and
Berber Jin
Listen
(1 min)
The OpenAI DevDay 2026 conference this week in San Francisco. Jeff Chiu / Associated Press
OpenAI has fired three researchers for alleged misconduct, including sharing confidential company information with a third-party AI-safety organization, according to people familiar with the matter.
The company recently told some employees that it had terminated three researchers who worked on its safety team, one of the people said.
