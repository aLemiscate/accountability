Starting June 15, paid Claude plans include a monthly Claude Agent SDK credit.
It covers usage on your own scripts and agents, claude -p, and third-party apps built on the SDK (OpenClaw, Conductor, etc) and it's separate from your regular usage limits. https://t.co/HT6R8EcE5z

Quoting https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2054610156214956455:
Monthly credit amounts vary by plan:

Pro: $20
Max 5x: $100
Max 20x: $200
Team Standard: $20/seat
Team Premium: $100/seat
Enterprise: Varies by seat type

After you claim the credit, it resets with each billing cycle. Credits do not rollover.
