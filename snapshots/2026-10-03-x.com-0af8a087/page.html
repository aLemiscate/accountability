Alex Albert on X: "Starting June 15, paid Claude plans include a monthly Claude Agent SDK credit. It covers usage on your own scripts and agents, claude -p, and third-party apps built on the SDK (OpenClaw, Conductor, etc) and it's separate from your regular usage limits."

Starting June 15, paid Claude plans include a monthly Claude Agent SDK credit. It covers usage on your own scripts and agents, claude -p, and third-party apps built on the SDK (OpenClaw, Conductor, etc) and it's separate from your regular usage limits.
