ChatGPT Business - Models & Limits
Updated: 2 months ago
Note: As of August 29th, 2025, we've renamed ChatGPT Team to ChatGPT Business. For more information and questions related to the name change, please refer to our article: ChatGPT Business Rename FAQ
The ChatGPT Business subscription is a self-serve subscription plan designed for organizations and businesses wishing to adopt ChatGPT for use among their teams. ChatGPT Business is available for 2 or more users. If you're looking for a larger option, please consider ChatGPT Enterprise.
If you are on ChatGPT flexible pricing, please refer to this page for latest rates on our models, features and products.
Model Picker
In the model picker, Auto automatically switches between Instant and Thinking, and you can also choose additional GPT-5 options directly from the top of the model picker.
• Instant — instant answers
• Thinking — deeper reasoning
• Pro — research-grade intelligence
Workspace owners can enable additional models (Thinking mini, o3, o4-mini, and GPT-4.1) for the entire workspace in their admin settings . GPT-4o is available in the model picker under Legacy Models by default.
Please note that the context window for Instant is 32K and for Thinking and Pro is 196K.
Note on Unlimited Access
The ChatGPT Business plan offers virtually unlimited GPT-5 Instant messages. However, usage must adhere to our Services Agreement , which prohibits, among other things:
• Abusive usage, such as automatically or programmatically extracting data.
• Sharing your account credentials or making your account available to anyone else.
• Reselling access or using ChatGPT to power third-party services.
We have guardrails in place to help prevent misuse and are always working to improve our systems. This may occasionally involve a temporary restriction on your usage. We will inform you when this happens, and if you think this might be a mistake, please don’t hesitate to reach out to our support team. If policy-violating behavior is not found, your access will be restored.
