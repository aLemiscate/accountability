Legacy Model Access for Team, Enterprise, and Edu Users
Updated: 6 days ago
With GPT-5, all Team and Enterprise workspaces are automatically using our newest models for a faster, more capable ChatGPT experience. If your workflows rely on a previous model, you can turn on legacy model access for a limited transition period. This lets you continue using select older models while you adapt to GPT-5, update custom GPTs, or wrap up ongoing projects before moving fully to the new defaults.
Legacy models available:
• GPT-4o
• OpenAI o3
• OpenAI o3 Pro
• GPT-4.1
• GPT-4.5
These models will appear in the model picker only if legacy model access is turned on for your workspace or account.
Please note that legacy models are still subject to credits and limits .
Enabling access to your workspace
ChatGPT workspace admins can enable access to legacy models in the workspace admin settings .
To enable:
• Click on your profile icon on the bottom-right of the screen.
​
• Select Workspace settings in the pop-up menu.
​
• Under Models, toggle on Enable legacy models to enable access to legacy models in your workspace.
Once legacy models have been enabled, they will appear in the model selector for users in your workspace.
