GPT-5.3 and GPT-5.4 in ChatGPT
Updated: 2 days ago
As of February 13, 2026, models GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini, OpenAI o4-mini, and GPT-5 (Instant and Thinking) have been retired from ChatGPT and are no longer available. API access remains unchanged.
For more information, please refer to our article: Retiring GPT-4o and other ChatGPT models
Please note that GPT-5.3 Instant is rolling out to all ChatGPT users over the next few days.
GPT-5.3 in ChatGPT is the default for all logged-in users. Auto is a single auto-switching system that brings together the best of our models into a smart, fast single experience.
Please note that GPT-5.3 Instant and GPT-5.4 Thinking and Pro are default off for ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu workspaces - Admins can enable it via the Early Model Access toggle in workspace settings in the “Models” section.
• GPT-5.3 Instant is a fast and powerful workhorse for everyday work and learning, with clear improvements in info-seeking questions, how-tos and walk-throughs, technical writing, and translation, all while retaining a warmer, more conversational tone.
• GPT-5.4 Thinking is our most capable reasoning model in ChatGPT and is designed for difficult, real-world work. Compared with earlier Thinking models, it is stronger at spreadsheet creation and editing, polished frontend code, slideshow creation, hard math, document understanding, instruction following, image understanding, tool use, and research tasks that require combining information from many sources on the web.
GPT-5.4 Thinking can think longer on hard tasks without timing out and does a better job keeping track of what it has already done, so you do not need to repeat important details as often. It may show a short preamble before reasoning starts, and you can add instructions while it is thinking to adjust direction before the final answer is complete. Outputs are also more streamlined, with cleaner formatting and less unnecessary header text.
• GPT-5.4 Pro is the highest-capability GPT-5.4 option in ChatGPT for the hardest tasks and long-running workflows.
How it works
When you select Auto in ChatGPT, ChatGPT can automatically decide whether to use GPT-5.3 Instant or GPT-5.4 Thinking for your request. For more complex tasks, Auto may switch to GPT-5.4 Thinking and apply deeper reasoning before answering.
When GPT-5.4 Thinking or GPT-5.4 Pro starts reasoning, it may begin with a short preamble that explains what it plans to do before it starts working. You can also add instructions while the model is still thinking to steer the response before it finishes.
If you manually select Thinking , ChatGPT will show a Thinking trace even for short reasoning sequences. If Auto routes your request to Thinking, ChatGPT may not always show a Thinking trace when the reasoning is brief.
You can also choose the model directly from the model picker:
• Auto — automatically switches between GPT-5.3 Instant and GPT-5.4 Thinking
• Instant — instant answers (GPT-5.3 Instant)
• Thinking — deeper reasoning (GPT-5.4 Thinking)
• Pro — research-grade intelligence (GPT-5.4 Pro)
Apps, Memory, Canvas, and image generation are not available with Pro.
Availability per ChatGPT Tier
GPT-5.3 is available to all ChatGPT tiers. Users on paid tiers—Plus, Pro, and Business—have access to the model picker, which enables you to manually select GPT-5.3 Instant or GPT-5.4 Thinking . GPT-5.4 Pro is only available to Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu plans.
Usage Limits
Free
ChatGPT Free tier accounts can send up to 10 messages with GPT-5.3 every 5 hours. After reaching this limit, chats will automatically use the mini version of the model until your limit resets.
Plus/Go
ChatGPT Plus and ChatGPT Go users can send up to 160 messages with GPT-5.3 every 3 hours. After reaching this limit, chats will switch to the mini version of the model until the limit resets.
Thinking usage
If you’re on Plus or Business, you can manually select GPT-5.4 Thinking from the model picker with a usage limit of up to 3,000 messages per week. Once you reach the weekly limit, you’ll see a pop-up notification, and GPT-5.4 Thinking will no longer be selectable from the menu.
If you’re on a Go plan, you can select Thinking from the tools menu by clicking the + icon in the chat entry box. Go users can send up to 10 messages every 5 hours after enabling Thinking.
Automatic switching from GPT-5.3 Instant to GPT-5.4 Thinking does not count toward this weekly limit, and ChatGPT may still switch to GPT-5.4 Thinking after you’ve reached it.
Business and Pro
The ChatGPT Business and Pro plans offer unlimited access to our GPT-5 models, subject to abuse guardrails. Usage must adhere to our Terms of Use, which prohibits, among other things:
• Abusive usage, such as automatically or programmatically extracting data.

Legacy models, GPT‑5, and old conversations
By default, all users have access to GPT-5.3 models.
As of February 13, 2026, ChatGPT retired GPT-5 (Instant and Thinking) as previously announced .
Because older chats now run on GPT-5.3 and GPT-5.4 equivalents, outputs may differ when you continue them.GPT-5.2 Thinking will remain available in Legacy Models for 90 days after the launch of GPT-5.4 Thinking for Plus and Pro users.
Legacy models in Enterprise and Edu
For the current list of legacy models available in Enterprise and Edu workspaces, please refer to our article: Legacy Model Access for Enterprise and Edu
Toggle thinking time in the message composer
When you select GPT‑5.4 Thinking in ChatGPT, you’ll see a thinking‑time toggle in the message composer.
• Standard (new default, balancing speed and intelligence) and Extended (the previous default for Plus) are available to all Plus and Business users.
• Pro users get two additional options: Light (snappiest) and Heavy (deeper reasoning).
All queries default to Standard unless you choose to change it. Once you choose a different thinking time, your preference is saved and used for future queries until you switch again.
You can decide what you need in the moment:
• Light / Standard → quicker answers that still apply reasoning, balancing speed and intelligence.
• Extended / Heavy → more time for deeper, more comprehensive responses when the stakes are higher or the question is more difficult.
Please note that this is only available on ChatGPT Web. The setting you choose on the web will not sync to mobile.

ChatGPT includes updated controls for tone and style:
• ChatGPT now has refined personality presets such as Default , Friendly (formerly Listener), Efficient (formerly Robot), Professional , Candid , and Quirky , with Cynical and Nerdy available under Personalization settings.
Learn more about customizing your ChatGPT personality.
• Controls under Personalization let you adjust how concise, warm, or scannable responses are, and how frequently emojis are used.
• Changes you make in Personalization apply across all chats immediately, including ongoing conversations. These personalization options are rolling out gradually and will continue to improve over time.
• GPT-5.3 and GPT-5.4 are also better at following Custom Instructions, giving you more precise control over tone and behavior
