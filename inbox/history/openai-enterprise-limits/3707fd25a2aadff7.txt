ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu - Models & Limits
Understand which models are best suited for your tasks.
Updated: 9 days ago
As of February 13, 2026, models GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini, OpenAI o4-mini, and GPT-5 (Instant and Thinking) have been retired from ChatGPT and are no longer available. API access remains unchanged.
For more information, please refer to our article: Retiring GPT-4o and other ChatGPT models
Overview
ChatGPT Enterprise is a subscription plan which offers enterprise-grade security and privacy, unlimited messages with GPT-5.2, native tools like apps, deep research, data analysis, file uploads, canvas, projects, search, advanced voice, and image generation, customization options, and much more.
Learn how ChatGPT can transform your organization.
If you are on ChatGPT flexible pricing, please refer to this page for latest rates on our models, features and products.
Please note that access to GPT-5.2 is by default disabled for ChatGPT Enterprise workspaces. Admins and owners can enable access in their workspace settings .
Model Picker
In the model picker, Auto automatically switches between Instant and Thinking, and you can also choose additional GPT-5.1 options directly from the top of the model picker.
• Auto — flagship model
• Instant — answers right away
• Thinking — get more thorough answers
• Pro — research-grade intelligence
Workspace admins can enable additional legacy models (including GPT-4o) for the entire workspace in their admin settings . For workspaces utilizing flexible pricing, please refer to the ChatGPT Rate Card for information on credit consumption by model.
Please note that the context window for GPT-5.2 is 128K and for GPT-5.2 Thinking is 196K.
For additional information on what legacy models are currently available, please refer to our article: Legacy Model Access for Enterprise and Edu
Managing GPT-5.2 Auto routing for reasoning (flexible pricing)
Enterprises on the flexible pricing plan can set GPT-5.2 Auto to use GPT-5.2 Thinking mini instead of GPT-5.2 Thinking for reasoning tasks. No credits are consumed when using GPT-5.2 Thinking mini.
Please note that GPT-5.2 already has light adaptive reasoning for tougher questions while staying fast
To update this setting:
• Go to Workspace settings .
• Under Models , enable Redirect GPT-5.2 Auto Router to Thinking mini
When turned on, reasoning requests sent to GPT-5.2 Auto will use GPT-5.2 Thinking mini instead of GPT-5.2 Thinking .
If GPT-5.2 Thinking is enabled in your workspace, members can still manually select it from the model picker.
Note on Unlimited Access
The ChatGPT Enterprise plan offers virtually unlimited GPT-5.2 messages. However, usage must adhere to our Services Agreement , which prohibits, among other things:
• Abusive usage, such as automatically or programmatically extracting data.
• Sharing your account credentials or making your account available to anyone else.
• Reselling access or using ChatGPT to power third-party services.
We have guardrails in place to help prevent misuse and are always working to improve our systems. This may occasionally involve a temporary restriction on your usage. We will inform you when this happens, and if you think this might be a mistake, please don’t hesitate to reach out to our support team. If policy-violating behavior is not found, your access will be restored.
GPT-5.2 behavior with role-based access control (RBAC)
We updated our routing logic so GPT-5.1 is only used when access is granted through role-based access control (RBAC). This ensures members see the correct model options and error messages based on your workspace settings.
For workspaces that use RBAC:
• New chats: When GPT-5.2 is enabled through RBAC, new chats that use GPT-5 will automatically use the equivalent GPT-5.2 model (for example, GPT-5 Thinking becomes GPT-5.1 Thinking).
• Existing chats: If a member starts a chat with GPT-5.1 while RBAC access is enabled and an admin later turns off access, new messages in that chat will not send and will display: “Access denied. Please try using another model.”
This behavior applies to all ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu workspaces that use RBAC.
