A check of the whole record for errors, omissions and inconsistencies: every
entry read against its sources, every quote and title checked by machine,
statuses and receipts checked for consistency, the commitments inventory
re-verified, a search for events the record was missing, and the site, code
and docs checked. Fixes went in as they were found. This file says what was
checked, what was wrong, what changed, and what is still open.
